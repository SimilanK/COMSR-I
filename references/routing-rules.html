DocInner copper on this board is 0.5 oz, so PYRO+, the firing drains and battery current stay on F.Cu / B.Cu. The table has no 0.5 oz column, so that value is left blank.

Thou ↔ mm

Tut p4–5Use thou for tracks, pads, spacing and grids. Use mm for holes and board outline. 100 thou = 2.54 mm; 1.27 mm = 50 thou.

Vias and clearance

Check a via against the pad-to-hole rule, then look up the spacing the tutorial's table asks for at a given voltage.

Pad-to-hole check

Tut p7Pad at least 1.8× the hole, or at least 0.5 mm larger. This matters most for vias, because drill shift can break out the pad.

Tut p11For high current use several vias between layers.

Tut p8General floor: 15 thou (0.38 mm) for basic through-hole, 10 or 8 thou for dense SMD. Stay above your manufacturer's minimum. Mains needs 8 mm minimum and the relevant standards.
