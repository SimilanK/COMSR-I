Ratsnest length 6.1 / 83.9 mm

Issues

  • FIRE_C (pin 7) and FIRE_D (pin 9) sit on both sides of LSE_IN (pin 8). Every fire edge lands next to the 32 kHz input.
  • The FIRE_C/FIRE_D ratsnest runs over X1 on its way to Q2.
  • Q2 is 6.6 mm from X1. JP1, where all firing return current joins GND, is 4.1 mm from Y1.
  • CubeMX allows only GPIO_SPEED_FREQ_LOW on PC13, PC14 and PC15. Every other pin allows all speeds. It still drives the gate through 1 kΩ, just with slower edges.

Fix direction

  • Move FIRE_C/FIRE_D off PC13/PC15 to free South-side GPIO (PE7 #38, PE8 #39, PE10 #41 are free).
  • Keep the West side for clocks, reset and analog only. No pyro part should face it.
  • Move JP1 to the battery connector and get Q2/CN2 off the West edge.

Ratsnest length 83.9 / 170.1 mm

Issues

  • The fire pins come from two sides: FIRE_A on South (pin 26), FIRE_B/C/D on West (pins 25, 7, 9).
  • The two FETs sit on different edges: Q1 bottom-centre, Q2 left edge mid-board.
  • Gate lines cross pyro power links 10 times. Gate signals have to cut across drain and PYRO+ copper.
  • FIRE_A/FIRE_B run 21–25 mm to Q1, about 2.7 mm past the IMU.

Fix direction

  • Put all four fire pins on one MCU side, the side facing the pyro edge.
  • Put both FETs on that edge with their gate side facing the MCU and their drain side facing the connector, so gate and drain copper never cross.

Ratsnest length 170.1 mm

Issues

  • PYRO+ links total 88 mm: the net spans J6 (left), CN1 (bottom) and CN2 (left).
  • C31/C32 firing bulk caps are at J6, about 21 mm from Q2 and 54 mm from Q1.
  • PYRO+ is also poured on In2.Cu under most of the board (next slide).
  • Pyro power links pass over J2 (BEC) and cross the servo links 6 times.

Fix direction

  • Bulk caps within 5 mm of the FETs.

Issues

  • Servo links cross the IMU bus 10 times, and the SERVO ratsnest runs straight over U9.

  • Servo links also cross USART3 (4) and pyro power (6).

Planes15 / 17

PYRO+ on the inner power layer

  • Two PYRO+ zones on In2.Cu. The large one covers x 121–160, y 74–126.
  • U1, U9, X1, Y1, FB1 and the VDDA caps all sit over it. The firing pulse would run in a plane under the clocks and IMU.
  • Inner copper is 0.5 oz, against the board's own rule that pyro stays on outer layers.
  • No GND zone on In1 yet.

Fix direction

  • Delete both In2 PYRO+ zones.
  • In1 = solid GND across the whole board.
  • In2 = +3V3 under the MCU and sensors, plus a small +5V island at the regulators.
  • PYRO+ and PYRO_GND on F.Cu/B.Cu, inside the pyro strip only.
Proposal16 / 17

Compartment plan from the fixed pins

Quiet end · short edge

Clocks at the West pins. Baro and SD at the North / North-East corner. USB and SWD at the East.

MCU

West = clocks, reset, VDDA, ADC only. East = USB, SWD, SD D0/D1, both UARTs (USART3 → PD8/PD9). North = SD + baro (CS → PD0/PD5).

South pins face the pyro edge

All four FIRE pins (FIRE_C/D → PE7/PE8). Servo TIM1 stays. IMU: Option A stays here ≥ 20 mm from the FETs, Option B moves to North with SPI1 on PB3–PB5 + SDMMC1.

Pyro + power edge · short edge

J6, arm/fuse entry, JP1 at J6 GND, Q1, Q2, CN1, CN2, C31/C32, SW3/SW4, J2, buck + LDO.

Notes17 / 17

Corrections to the earlier review

  • Diodes: withdrawn as an issue.
  • Buzzer: dropped from this board. The COTS flight computer's beeper covers audible arming checks.
  • PC13 / PC15 "weak pins": the evidence is CubeMX's GPIO database: PC13, PC14 and PC15 accept only GPIO_SPEED_FREQ_LOW. They will switch the MOSFET through 1 kΩ. The stronger reason to move FIRE_C/D is that they straddle LSE_IN on the clock side. ST's datasheet footnote on these pins (backup-domain power switch, limited current) could not be opened from here. Check the pin-definition table notes in the STM32U575 datasheet.
  • Routing: most of the board is unrouted. Every crossing in this deck is a ratsnest crossing to plan around, not a copper fault.
