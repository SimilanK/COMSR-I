U1 sits rotated 45° clockwise, so each original side faces a board corner: West → NW (clocks, reset, analog, telemetry UART), South → SW (IMU, fire, servo), East → SE (USB, SWD, companion UART, SD), North → NE (SD, baro, BOOT0). Pin functions come from the CubeMX database (STM32U575VGTx.xml); "now" comes from Main board.kicad_pcb. Check this plan before editing Mainboard.ioc.

Floorplan · U1 rotated 45° clockwise

At 45° clockwise, pin 1 sits at the top corner. The original West side (1–25) faces north-west, South (26–50) faces south-west, East (51–75) faces south-east, North (76–100) faces north-east. Each face points at the block it serves: clocks and analog to the quiet top-left, IMU and pyro lines down-left, USB/SWD/power down-right, SD card in the NE corner with the baro on the top edge.   • Why 45° helps: every face now looks at a board corner. The SW face (fire, servo, IMU) points straight at the pyro strip, and the SE face (USB, SWDIO) points at USB-C and power.
  • Actuation pins: #42–47 are six neighbouring pins in connector order (A, B, SERVO1, SERVO2, D, C). Left-going signals use the free space above the IMU-to-pyro gap; right-going ones go down a corridor between J2 and the power block. The full route is drawn in the pyro edge section.
  • SD card: SDMMC2 would put D0/D1 (#53/54) at the S corner, far from CK/CMD/D2/D3 (#87–90). SDMMC1 keeps all six around the E corner, next to the card, and frees PB3 for SWO.
  • Baro stays on SPI2: its pins (#81–85) are mid-NE, so the baro sits at the top edge. One catch: SDMMC1 CMD is #83, between SPI2 SCK #82 and MISO #84, so one SD or SPI trace has to cross. Both are slow, so a short hop on B.Cu is fine.
  • UARTs: USART1 stays on PA9/PA10 for J7. Only the telemetry link moves, USART3 → UART4 on PA0/PA1, which sits at the W corner and exits straight left to J8. PA2/PA3 (#25/26) stay free.
  • Decoupling: at 45° the VDD/VSS caps sit on the diagonal faces. Keep them on the pins as in your screenshot. They make the chip footprint larger, so leave about 3 mm around the diamond before placing other blocks.

Pyro edge layout · fed from the floorplan

The six actuation signals leave U1's south-west face on six neighbouring pins, #42–47, in the same left-to-right order as the connectors: FIRE_A, FIRE_B, SERVO1, SERVO2, FIRE_D, FIRE_C. A, B and SERVO1 fan left; SERVO2, D and C pass under U1's south corner, go down a corridor right of J2, and fan right below the power block. No signal crosses another or crosses BEC+.   • The three servo wires sit together (CN1 pins 3–5, CN2 pins 2–4) but in GND / SERVO / V+ order. A standard servo plug has V+ in the middle, so the servo leads get landed one by one in the screw block, not plugged.
  • FET channels already match this order in the current schematic: Q1 left channel = FIRE_A → CN1 pin 1, right = FIRE_B → pin 2; Q2 left = FIRE_D → CN2 pin 5, right = FIRE_C → pin 6. Gate resistors R36/R35 and R38/R37 sit in the same order.
  • The PYRO+ bar (y ≈ 116.4) and the V+ select legs pass within ~1 mm of each other near the JP pads. Keep both on F.Cu with the Pyro / Servo net-class widths and check clearance when routing.
  • The fan-out pins are 0.35 mm apart at 45°, so in real copper the six tracks leave the pins diagonally first and spread out before turning down. Keep them on F.Cu over solid GND (In1).
